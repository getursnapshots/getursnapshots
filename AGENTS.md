# Custom Instructions

## Brand Guidelines for Snap Shots

Buttons use the signature brand red (#bd1616) with white text.

### Button & Color System:
- **Button Primary**: `#bd1616` (Hover: `#9e1212`, Active/Deep: `#750d0d`, Text: `#FFFFFF`)
- **Black**: `#000000`
- **White**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Accent/Highlights**: Yellow/Gold `#ffc800`

### Architecture & Design Rules:
- Recreate the browsing experience and visual rhythm of the reference layout without copying copyrighted assets.
- Maintain the exact section order, spacing, typography hierarchy, and card layouts.
- Apply button colors (#bd1616 with white text, hover: #9e1212, active: #750d0d) consistently to all action buttons and CTAs.
