# Snap Shots Video Reels Directory

Place your MP4 video files into this directory (`/public/videos/`).

## Recommended Filenames:
- `reel-1.mp4` (Displayed on Slide 1)
- `reel-2.mp4` (Displayed on Slide 2)
- `reel-3.mp4` (Displayed on Slide 3)
- `reel-4.mp4` (Displayed on Slide 4)
- `reel-5.mp4` (Displayed on Slide 5)
- `reel-6.mp4` (Displayed on Slide 6)
- `reel-7.mp4` (Displayed on Slide 7)
- `reel-8.mp4` (Displayed on Slide 8)
- `reel-9.mp4` (Displayed on Slide 9)
- `reel-10.mp4` (Displayed on Slide 10)

## Video Specifications:
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264 / AAC)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 vertical (recommended 1080x1920)
- **Delivery**: Automatically bundled into production build at `/videos/[filename].mp4`
